name: initials
description: Extract the dotted initials of a full name.
---
Skill: initials extractor.

The initials of a full name are the first letter of each word, written in uppercase,
separated by dots, with a trailing dot and no spaces.

Procedure: take the first character of every word in the name, uppercase it, and join
the characters with dots, adding a dot at the end.

Worked example: "ada lovelace" gives "A.L."; "john von neumann" gives "J.V.N.".
