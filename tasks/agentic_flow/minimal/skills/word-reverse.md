---
name: word-reverse
description: Reverse the letters of a single word.
---
Skill: word reverser.

To reverse a word, write its letters in the opposite order: the last letter first
and the first letter last. Keep everything lowercase and do not add spaces.

Procedure: read the word from right to left and write the letters down in that order.

Worked example: reversing "stressed" gives "desserts"; reversing "regal" gives
"lager".
